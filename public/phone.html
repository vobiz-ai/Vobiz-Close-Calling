      Frequently asked

      Why does the softphone run in its own tab?

      Close has no panel where another calling service can hold a live call, so the phone lives in this tab. Pin it and keep it open during your shift.

      How does a call connect?

      Your browser places the call: it connects to Vobiz first, and Vobiz then dials the customer from your chosen caller ID. You hear the customer's phone ringing, and the call is bridged as soon as they answer.

      Why is the Call button disabled?

      Calling needs a Close connection, a Vobiz sign-in and a live SIP registration. The hint under the button says which one is missing.

      Where are calls logged?

      Every call, answered or not, becomes a Call activity on the caller's lead in Close, with its direction, status and talk time. An unknown number becomes a new lead. A missed inbound call also gets a "Call back" task. The recording link is added to the call when it is ready.

      Why does clicking a number in Close ask before it dials?

      Any website can open a tel: link, so the softphone always shows the number and waits for you to click Call.
